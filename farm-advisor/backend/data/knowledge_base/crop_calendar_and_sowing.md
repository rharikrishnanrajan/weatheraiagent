# Crop Sowing Calendar & Germination Soil Temperature Guide

## Overview
Proper sowing and transplanting timing depends fundamentally on soil temperature, soil moisture status, and post-sowing weather forecasts. Planting into cold, waterlogged soil leads to seed decay, stunted root development, and uneven stands.

## Crop-Specific Sowing Thresholds

### 1. Tomatoes (*Solanum lycopersicum*)
- **Minimum Soil Temperature:** 15°C (59°F). Optimal: 20°C to 28°C (68°F - 82°F).
- **Sowing / Transplant Window:** Spring after all danger of frost has passed (soil consistently > 16°C at 5 cm depth).
- **Sowing Depth:** 0.5 cm for seed nursery beds; transplants set deep (up to the first true leaves) to encourage adventitious root formation.
- **Weather Window Requirement:** 5 consecutive frost-free days with sunny conditions and night temperatures staying above 10°C.
- **Critical Risk:** Night temperatures below 8°C severely delay vegetative growth and induce blossom drop on young transplants.

### 2. Corn / Maize (*Zea mays*)
- **Minimum Soil Temperature:** 10°C (50°F) for standard field corn; 15°C (60°F) for supersweet sweet corn cultivars.
- **Germination Speed:** 10-12 days at 10-12°C; 4-6 days at 18-22°C.
- **Sowing Depth:** 4 to 5 cm in loamy/silt soils; 5 to 6 cm in light sandy soils to reach uniform moisture.
- **Weather Window Requirement:** Avoid planting 24-48 hours before an intense cold rain event. Cold imbibition injury occurs if seed imbibes water colder than 8°C within the first 36 hours.

### 3. Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*)
- **Winter Wheat:** Sow when mean soil temperature drops to 12°C - 16°C in autumn. Allows 4-6 weeks of vegetative growth and crown development before freezing dormancy.
- **Spring Wheat:** Sow as soon as soil thaws and drains to field capacity in early spring (soil temp 4°C - 7°C).
- **Sowing Depth:** 3 to 4 cm. Sowing deeper than 5 cm causes weak coleoptile emergence and delayed tillering.
- **Weather Window Requirement:** Moderate soil moisture; avoid sowing into saturated muck.

### 4. Potatoes (*Solanum tuberosum*)
- **Minimum Soil Temperature:** 8°C (46°F) at 10 cm depth. Optimal: 12°C to 18°C.
- **Planting Depth:** 10 to 15 cm with furrow ridges.
- **Weather Window Requirement:** Avoid waterlogged soils. Seed pieces decay rapidly if soil remains water-saturated for > 48 hours at planting.

### 5. Soybean (*Glycine max*)
- **Minimum Soil Temperature:** 13°C (55°F) with warming trend.
- **Sowing Depth:** 2.5 to 3.5 cm.
- **Weather Window Requirement:** Dry topsoil layer; avoid planting directly before heavy rain causing soil crusting.

Source: International Agronomy Extension Service - Sustainable Cropping Guidelines.
