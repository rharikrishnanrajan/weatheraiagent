# Fertilizer Scheduling & Weather-Aligned Nutrient Application

## The 4R Nutrient Stewardship Framework
Applying the **Right Source**, at the **Right Rate**, at the **Right Time**, in the **Right Place** is heavily dependent on real-time weather and rainfall forecasts.

## Weather Rules for Nitrogen Application
1. **Never Apply Urea or Ammonium-Nitrate Before Heavy Rains (> 20 mm):**
   - Nitrate ($NO_3^-$) is highly mobile in water. Heavy rainfall causes deep leaching below the root zone into groundwater, resulting in financial loss and environmental pollution.
2. **Volatilization Risk Under High Temperatures:**
   - Unincorporated surface-applied urea under warm temperatures (> 25°C) and moist alkaline soils hydrolyzes rapidly into ammonia gas ($NH_3$). Up to 30% of applied nitrogen can volatilize into the atmosphere within 5 days.
   - *Mitigation:* Incorporate mechanically within 4 hours, apply immediately prior to light gentle rain (5-10 mm), or use urease inhibitors (NBPT).
3. **Denitrification in Waterlogged Soils:**
   - Saturated, anaerobic warm soils encourage anaerobic bacteria to convert nitrate into nitrous oxide ($N_2O$) and nitrogen gas, exhausting soil fertility.

## Crop-Specific Nutritional Milestones
- **Corn / Maize:**
  - *Basal at Planting:* Moderate starter nitrogen, phosphorus (for early root proliferation), and zinc.
  - *Side-Dress (V6 to V8 Stage):* Major nitrogen demand occurs during rapid vegetative elongation. Apply side-dress N when soil is moist and workable, not during peak drought or waterlogging.
- **Tomatoes:**
  - High potassium ($K^+$) and calcium ($Ca^{2+}$) required during flowering and fruit expansion.
  - Calcium uptake is purely transpirational: severe humidity swings or drought cause localized deficiency leading to blossom end rot. Maintain steady soil moisture.
- **Wheat:**
  - Split nitrogen: 1/3 at crown root initiation, 1/3 at active tillering, and 1/3 at jointing. Late foliar nitrogen during flag leaf/booting increases grain protein content.

## Foliar Micronutrient Weather Guidelines
- Apply foliar Zinc, Boron, or Iron when relative humidity is between 60% and 80%, wind is < 10 km/h, and temperature is 18°C to 24°C.
- Avoid spraying foliar fertilizers in full midday sun to prevent salt burn on tender leaves.

Source: International Fertilizer Management Protocol & Agronomic Soil Science Guide.
