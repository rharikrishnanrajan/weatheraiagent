# Integrated Pest & Fungal Disease Management Guide

## Weather-Disease Interaction Mechanics
Fungal spores and bacterial pathogens require two critical environmental catalysts for germination and host penetration:
1. **Prolonged Free Moisture / Leaf Wetness:** Usually >= 6 to 12 consecutive hours of dew, rain, or mist.
2. **Pathogen-Permissive Temperatures:** Most crop fungi proliferate actively between 15°C and 28°C (59°F - 82°F).

## Key Diseases and Intervention Thresholds

### 1. Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*) - Tomatoes and Potatoes
- **Environmental Trigger:** Temperatures between 12°C and 22°C combined with relative humidity > 85% for at least 8 hours (Smith Periods or Blight Units).
- **Damage Profile:** Entire fields can collapse within 7 to 10 days if unchecked. Brown water-soaked leaf margins with white downy mildew underneath.
- **Cultural Control:** Wide plant spacing for airflow; staking and indeterminate trellis pruning; strictly avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation.
- **Spray Strategy:** Prophylactic copper fungicides (copper oxychloride / copper hydroxide) or biologicals (*Bacillus amyloliquefaciens*) applied *before* the wet storm front arrives. Once sporulation is active, use systemic curative oomycides (mancozeb + cymoxanil or dimethomorph).

### 2. Early Blight (*Alternaria solani*) - Solanaceous Crops
- **Environmental Trigger:** Moderate to high temperatures (20°C - 30°C) with alternating wet and dry periods.
- **Damage Profile:** Concentric target-board brown circular spots on oldest lower leaves.
- **Management:** Mulch bare soil to eliminate raindrop splash from soil inoculum; remove bottom foliage up to 30 cm off ground.

### 3. Rust Diseases (Stripe Rust, Leaf Rust, Common Rust)
- **Crops Affected:** Wheat, Barley, Corn.
- **Environmental Trigger:** High humidity or heavy nighttime dews. Stripe rust favors cool weather (10-15°C); Common corn rust favors 16-25°C.
- **Management:** Scout flag leaves during stem elongation. If pustules appear on > 5% of leaf area before flowering, apply triazole (propiconazole / tebuconazole) fungicide during safe spray weather windows.

### 4. Insect Pest Alerts & Spray Windows
- **Aphids, Whiteflies, Thrips:** Vector viruses (e.g. Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus). Multiply rapidly during dry, warm spells (25°C - 32°C). Apply neem oil (azadirachtin) or insecticidal soap during late evening to avoid killing beneficial honeybees and pollinators.
- **Fall Armyworm & Caterpillars:** Active egg-laying on young corn leaves. Apply *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt kurstaki) during dusk when larvae emerge to feed on fresh whorls.
